## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a joyful, tactile, and whimsical storybook aesthetic designed for creators, learners, and families across all ages. It blends a gentle hand-drawn cartoon charm with modern interface discipline—avoiding chaotic noise in favor of generous breathing room, crisp layout structure, and delightfully responsive touchpoints.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Cheerfully Welcoming:** Radiates warmth and optimism through sunny tones, soft edges, and friendly visual metaphors.
- **Tactile & Bubbly:** Every interactive element invites physical interaction, resembling sticker sheets, stamp pads, and bubbly clay buttons.
- **Clear & Uncluttered:** Uses generous whitespace and soft paper textures to ensure content stays effortless to read and navigate.

### Design Movement
- **Tactile Soft-Pop & Hand-Drawn Minimal:** Combines thick, pillowy outlines, offset "sticker" drop shadows, organic hand-inked borders, and soft candy pastels grounded by warm archival paper surfaces.

## Colors

The palette revolves around warm, candy-tinted pastels anchored by rich, deep charcoal ink rather than pure black. Surfaces mimic clean, creamy sketch paper, avoiding stark sterile whites.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF7A59` — Playful Coral Peach):** Main brand callouts, primary action states, and high-energy focal badges.
- **Secondary (`#FFC837` — Sunny Butter Yellow):** Highlights, celebration banners, active stars, and secondary chips.
- **Tertiary (`#4ECDC4` — Sky Cyan):** Fresh interactive anchors, informative badges, and complementary buttons.
- **Accent Lilac (`#B39DDB`):** Creative tools, category tagging, and whimsical micro-details.
- **Accent Bubblegum (`#FF85A1`):** Heart reactions, playful tags, and delightful alert notes.
- **Accent Grass Mint (`#7BDCB5`):** Success toasts, progress fills, and verified doodle badges.
- **Neutral Deep Ink (`#2B262D`):** Rich charcoal used for typography, borders, and expressive doodle strokes to keep contrast sharp while feeling like dark archival pen ink.
- **Canvas Cream (`#FFFDF7`):** Warm, inviting paper background that reduces eye strain and provides natural coziness.
- **Surface Paper Tint (`#FBF6EB`):** Container backgrounds and card baselines that establish soft depth without rigid drop shadows.

## Typography

The type scale combines the punchy, bubbly geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles and labels with the soft, friendly terminal curves of **Quicksand** for reading and descriptive copy.

### Usage Rules
- Titles and high-level headings always use bold to extra-bold weights (`700` and `800`) with snug line-heights to evoke playful cartoon titles.
- Body copy relies on Quicksand with elevated medium weights (`500` or `600`) to guarantee effortless legibility for young readers, seniors, and casual scrollers alike.
- Avoid all-caps transformations except on micro-labels or tiny badge tags, keeping the cadence gentle, conversational, and soft-spoken.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, open fluid grid that prioritizes breathing space and prevents visual clutter.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `0.75rem` column gutters. Components stack into single-column bubbly cards.
- **Tablet (641px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` outer canvas margin, `1rem` column gutters.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`, `2rem` outer canvas margin, `1.25rem` gutters.

### Rhythm
Components rely on loose, comfortable inner padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to prevent dense, cramped interfaces. Groups of interactive modules sit apart using `space-lg`, ensuring tap targets remain generous and accidental clicks are avoided.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects blurry realism and dark atmospheric shadows in favor of **tactile "sticker" depth** and bold, ink-outlined elevation.

### Tactile Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ink):** Clean surface sitting directly on `#FFFDF7` paper with a soft `2px solid #2B262D` outline or subtle `#F5EDE0` fill.
- **Level 1 (Resting Stickers):** Surface layered with a crisp offset shadow: `0px 4px 0px #2B262D` alongside a `2.5px solid #2B262D` border.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Floating Objects):** Surfaces rise slightly: `0px 6px 0px #2B262D` with an upward transform of `-2px`.
- **Level 3 (Pressed State):** Buttons and interactives press down: shadow collapses to `0px 0px 0px #2B262D` with a downward transform of `+4px`, providing instant, satisfying haptic visual feedback.
- **Tinted Backdrops:** Modal screens and drawer curtains utilize a translucent warm milky cream (`rgba(43, 38, 45, 0.35)`) rather than harsh cold black.

## Shapes

Shapes across the system are ultra-rounded, soft, and bouncy. The visual language evokes pebble forms, round-corner sketchpads, and puffy stickers.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Standard for buttons, tags, chips, search inputs, and navigation toggles.
- **Bubbly Containers (`rounded-3xl` / 24px - 32px):** Used for cards, modal dialogs, and drawer sheets.
- **Playful Asymmetry:** Where appropriate, decorative badges and spotlight panels may feature slightly varied border radii (e.g., `rounded-tl-3xl rounded-br-3xl rounded-tr-xl rounded-bl-xl`) to mimic authentic hand-drawn paper cutouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), Coral Peach (`#FF7A59`) background, deep ink text (`#2B262D`), `2.5px solid #2B262D` border, and an offset hard shadow `0px 4px 0px #2B262D`.
- **Hover & Active Behavior:** Hover moves the button up `2px` with shadow expanding to `6px`. On `:active`, the button translates `+4px` down and shadow drops to `0px`, producing a physical squishy "thump."
- **Secondary Button:** Same mechanical border and tactile shadow, but filled with Cream or Butter Yellow (`#FFC837`).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with light pastel backgrounds (Mint `#7BDCB5`, Lilac `#B39DDB`, Bubblegum `#FF85A1`).
- Borders are thinner (`1.5px solid #2B262D`), pairing `label-sm` or `label-md` typography with cheerful doodle icon prefixes.

### Cards
- Backed by `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF6EB` set against the `#FFFDF7` canvas.
- Rounded by `24px` to `28px`, framed by a distinct `2px solid #2B262D` outline and `0px 4px 0px rgba(43, 38, 45, 0.12)` ambient sticker shadow.
- Ample inner padding (`space-lg`) to maintain a clean, airy sketchbook feeling.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Pill-shaped text fields with generous vertical height (minimum `52px` touch target).
- Cream background (`#FFFFFF`), `2px solid #2B262D` ink border, and warm placeholders in muted ink tones (`rgba(43, 38, 45, 0.45)`).
- On focus, stroke thickens slightly and casts an offset `0px 3px 0px #FF7A59` pastel glow ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Squishy squircle checkboxes (`rounded-lg`, `24px x 24px`) and round pebble radio toggles.
- Checked state reveals a hand-drawn doodle checkmark or solid pastel fill stamped inside a bold `2px` outline.

### Doodle Stickers & Canvas Stamps (Extended Component)
- Decorative badge stamps with whimsical tilt rotations (`-3deg` to `+4deg`).
- Dotted canvas borders and playful squiggly separator rules that replace rigid, clinical divider lines.
